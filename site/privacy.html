Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective 30 September 2026

RYRY does not collect any data. It has no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no account, and the developer runs no server that the app talks to.

What stays on your Mac

  • Your log, settings, received text and recordings are stored on your Mac only.
  • Passwords and API keys for online services are kept in the macOS Keychain.
  • The microphone (audio input) is used only to receive RTTY from your radio. Audio is not recorded unless you choose File → Record RX to WAV, and it is never sent anywhere.

Services you may turn on

These are off until you enable them and enter your own account. RYRY then connects to the service directly; their own privacy policies apply, and the developer receives nothing:

  • Callbook (QRZ.com, HamQTH) – the callsign you look up and your login.
  • Log upload (eQSL, Club Log) – the QSOs you upload and your login.
  • DX cluster and Reverse Beacon Network – your callsign as the login.
  • LoTW – through the separate TrustedQSL app, which you run yourself.
  • Logger API – a local interface for programs on your Mac (127.0.0.1) unless you allow remote access in Settings.

Children

RYRY does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
