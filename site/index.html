Features

Everything an RTTY station needs.

From the first CQ to the Cabrillo file — receive, transmit, log and score, with your radio on the USB cable and the Mac's sound card.

Rack A — Signal
Receive

MMTTY demodulator

  • IIR, FIR, PLL and FFT demodulators, AFC, ATC
  • Squelch, notch and LMS filters
  • Second decoder and multi-channel decoding
45.45–110 Bdany shift
Transmit

AFSK or FSK

  • AFSK through the sound card, FSK over a USB serial port
  • PTT by RTS/DTR or CAT
  • Macros F1–F12 with MMTTY variables
RTS/DTRCAT PTT
Tuning

Spectrum & waterfall

  • Click to tune, right-click to notch
  • XY cross indicator and a demodulator scope
  • Spots shown as labels in the waterfall
XY scopeband labels
